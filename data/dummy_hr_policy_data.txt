DUMMY COMPANY HR POLICY DOCUMENT
Company: ExampleTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd.
Document Type: Sample HR Policy
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 01 January 2026
Status: Fictional sample for learning and RAG project practice

IMPORTANT NOTICE
This document contains entirely fictional policies for demonstration and educational use.
It is not legal advice and does not represent any real employer's rules. Replace these
examples with approved company policies before using an HR chatbot in a real workplace.

============================================================
1. WORKING HOURS AND ATTENDANCE
============================================================
- Standard working days are Monday through Friday.
- Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM.
- Employees receive a 60-minute lunch break.
- Employees should record attendance using the company's attendance system.
- Employees who expect to arrive more than 15 minutes late should notify their manager.
- Flexible working arrangements require manager approval.
- Some teams may follow different schedules based on business needs.

Example question: What are the standard working hours?
Sample answer: The fictional standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday
through Friday, with a 60-minute lunch break.

============================================================
2. LEAVE POLICY
============================================================
- Full-time employees receive 12 days of casual leave per calendar year.
- Full-time employees receive 12 days of sick leave per calendar year.
- Full-time employees receive 18 days of earned leave per calendar year.
- Leave entitlements are prorated for eligible employees who join during the year.
- Employees should request planned leave through the HR portal at least 3 working
  days in advance.
- Sick leave should be reported to the manager as soon as reasonably possible.
- Leave approval depends on team coverage and the applicable approval workflow.
- Unused leave carry-forward rules are subject to the company's annual leave procedure.
- Employees should contact HR for leave balances or questions about eligibility.

Example question: How many earned leave days are provided?
Sample answer: This fictional policy provides eligible full-time employees with
18 days of earned leave per calendar year.

============================================================
3. REMOTE WORK AND HYBRID WORK
============================================================
- Employees may request remote work with their manager's approval.
- The sample hybrid arrangement is up to 3 remote working days per week.
- Team or client requirements may require employees to work from the office.
- Employees working remotely must protect company information and use approved devices.
- Employees must remain reachable during their agreed working hours.
- Remote work from another country requires written approval from HR and Information
  Security before the employee makes arrangements.

Example question: Can an employee work remotely?
Sample answer: Employees may request remote work. Manager approval and team requirements
apply under this fictional policy.

============================================================
4. HOLIDAYS
============================================================
- The company publishes a holiday calendar at the beginning of each calendar year.
- Holiday dates may vary by office location.
- Employees should check the HR portal for their location's approved holiday calendar.
- A team that must operate on a holiday may arrange compensatory time off according
  to the applicable internal procedure.

============================================================
5. CODE OF CONDUCT
============================================================
- Employees must treat colleagues, customers, and business partners respectfully.
- Harassment, discrimination, bullying, threats, and retaliation are prohibited.
- Employees should disclose potential conflicts of interest to HR or their manager.
- Company equipment and accounts should be used responsibly and in accordance with
  company policies.
- Employees must not falsify attendance, expense claims, work records, or approvals.
- Concerns may be reported to HR through the designated reporting channel.

============================================================
6. INFORMATION SECURITY AND CONFIDENTIALITY
============================================================
- Employees must protect confidential company, employee, and customer information.
- Passwords and multi-factor authentication codes must not be shared.
- Employees should use only approved software, storage, and file-sharing services.
- Suspected phishing messages or security incidents should be reported promptly to
  the IT or Information Security team.
- Confidential data must not be pasted into public AI tools unless the company has
  explicitly approved that use and the data handling is permitted.
- Employees must lock their screens when leaving devices unattended.
- Company data should be handled according to its classification and retention rules.

Example question: Can I paste confidential HR data into a public AI chatbot?
Sample answer: Not unless the company has explicitly approved the tool and the
specific data use under its security policies.

============================================================
7. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
============================================================
- Business expenses must have a valid business purpose.
- Employees should submit expense claims through the approved expense system within
  30 calendar days of the transaction.
- Receipts are required for expenses unless the expense procedure specifies an exception.
- Manager approval is required before reimbursement is processed.
- Personal purchases are not reimbursable.
- Travel bookings should follow the company's travel approval process.

============================================================
8. BUSINESS TRAVEL
============================================================
- Business travel must be approved by the employee's manager before booking.
- Employees should use approved booking channels where available.
- Travel claims must include required receipts and supporting documents.
- Employees should contact the travel desk or HR for accessibility or safety support.
- International travel may require additional security, visa, and compliance checks.

============================================================
9. PERFORMANCE AND DEVELOPMENT
============================================================
- Employees and managers should discuss goals at the beginning of the review cycle.
- The sample company conducts formal performance reviews twice per year.
- Feedback should be specific, respectful, and related to work expectations.
- Employees may request training or professional development through their manager.
- Promotion and compensation decisions follow separate internal review processes.

============================================================
10. ONBOARDING AND PROBATION
============================================================
- New employees receive onboarding instructions from HR.
- Employees must complete required policy, security, and compliance training.
- The sample probation period is 6 months, unless the offer letter states otherwise.
- The manager should provide feedback during the probation period.
- Questions about employment terms should be directed to HR.

============================================================
11. PARENTAL AND FAMILY LEAVE
============================================================
- Employees should contact HR for applicable parental, adoption, caregiver, or family
  leave benefits.
- Eligibility, duration, documentation, and pay depend on applicable law and the
  employer's approved policy.
- This fictional sample intentionally does not define legal entitlements.
- HR should provide the current approved policy for the employee's work location.

============================================================
12. GRIEVANCES AND WORKPLACE CONCERNS
============================================================
- Employees may raise workplace concerns with HR, their manager, or the designated
  ethics or reporting channel.
- Reports should include relevant dates, events, and supporting information where possible.
- Reports will be handled according to applicable company procedures and law.
- Retaliation against a person who raises a concern in good faith is prohibited under
  this fictional sample policy.
- If the concern involves the employee's manager, the employee may contact HR directly.

============================================================
13. RESIGNATION AND EXIT PROCESS
============================================================
- Employees who plan to resign should submit written notice using the approved process.
- The notice period is governed by the employment agreement and applicable policy.
- Employees must return company equipment and complete assigned handover tasks.
- HR will share exit formalities and final settlement steps.
- Employees should confirm their final working date with HR and their manager.

============================================================
14. PAYROLL AND PAYSLIPS
============================================================
- Payroll is processed monthly under the company's published payroll calendar.
- Employees should review their payslips and report discrepancies to Payroll or HR.
- Bank account changes must be submitted through the approved secure process.
- Salary details and payroll records are confidential personal information.
- Tax deductions and statutory contributions depend on applicable law and eligibility.

============================================================
15. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
============================================================
Q: Where can I check my leave balance?
A: Check the HR portal or contact HR if the balance appears incorrect.

Q: Who approves planned leave?
A: The employee's designated manager or the configured approval workflow approves leave.

Q: Can I install any software on my work laptop?
A: Use approved software and follow the company's IT and security procedures.

Q: What should I do if I receive a suspicious email?
A: Do not open suspicious links or attachments. Report the message to IT or Information
   Security using the approved reporting process.

Q: Where can I find office-specific holidays?
A: Check the current holiday calendar in the HR portal for your office location.

Q: How do I report a payroll error?
A: Contact Payroll or HR through the approved internal channel and provide the relevant
   pay period and details securely.

Q: Does this document represent a real company's official policy?
A: No. It is fictional sample data created for learning and project development.
